WHAT WAITS INSIDE

RUSH
When the lights start screaming, hide in a wardrobe or under a bed. Do not be in the open when it passes.
AMBUSH
It comes back. And back. Stay hidden until the lights are steady again.
SCREECH
A whisper in the dark. Find it with your eyes and stare until it breaks.
HALT
It blocks the hall and pulls toward you. Back away. Never let it touch you. Wait for the flash.
THE FIGURE
Door 50. It is blind, but it hears everything. Crouch, stay quiet, and hide under the library tables. Reach the elevator.

Checkpoints are lit every ten doors by the Guiding Light. Death returns you to the last one.
